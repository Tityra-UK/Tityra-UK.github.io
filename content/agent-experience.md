People increasingly ask AI to research a business, compare products or choose a service. An agent helping with those decisions needs information it can access and understand.

Agent Experience (AX) considers that interaction from the agent's perspective. A website is one part of a wider experience, alongside the other products and services an agent may use on someone's behalf.

## Why does AX matter for a business?

Your website explains what you offer. For an agent, the details behind that offer can matter as much as its headline: which product a description refers to, where a service is available or what conditions apply.

For example, a service may only be available in certain locations. If an agent misses that condition, it may give someone an incomplete picture of your business. Content that is clear to a visitor can still be difficult for an agent to interpret.

Agent readiness is a way to think about these needs. It is specific to the task and the information involved. Different agents can interact with the same website in different ways.
