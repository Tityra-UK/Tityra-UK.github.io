[Tityra](https://tityra.com/) helps businesses understand issues that can make their website content difficult for AI agents to access and understand.

Its website content audits provide documented findings and practical recommendations. These help the people responsible for a website identify what needs attention and plan changes.

## Who is Tityra for?

Tityra is for organisations whose websites explain their brands, products or services. This includes publishers, retailers, technology businesses, agencies, public sector organisations and financial services businesses.

The relevant questions depend on the business and the pages included in the audit. A product catalogue and a public information service have different needs.

This is Tityra's official GitHub Pages website. It offers a brief introduction to [Agent Experience](/agent-experience/) and agent readiness for website owners.
